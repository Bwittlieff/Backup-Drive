Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Purpose

Proxmox Rclone Backup is a personal application used to access Google Drive through Rclone for private server backup and file management.

Google Drive Data

The application accesses Google Drive files only as authorized by the account owner.

Files may be uploaded, downloaded, organized, or deleted at the account owner's direction.

Data Sharing

The application is not a public service. It does not sell or share personal data with third parties.

Data Storage and Security

Authentication credentials are stored locally in the owner's Rclone configuration. Backups are stored in the owner's Google Drive account.

Access Revocation

The account owner can revoke Google Drive access through Google Account settings.
